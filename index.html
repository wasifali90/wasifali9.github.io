01 / About

SEO with a clear
business purpose.

Good SEO is more than rankings. It connects the right search intent with useful pages and a clear next step.

I work across the parts of SEO that help a website get discovered, understood, and considered by the right audience. My work includes technical audits, keyword and search-intent research, content planning, on-page improvements, internal linking, and backlink acquisition.

My current portfolio spans pharmaceutical and health brands, e-commerce, healthcare services, and healthcare technology. I also work on a US-focused healthcare project where the goal is to support qualified inquiries and business opportunities, not just increase sessions.

I use Google Search Console, GA4, crawling tools, and competitor research to find issues, prioritize work, and measure what changes after implementation.

My approach: understand the search intent, fix the important issues, publish useful content, and measure outcomes honestly.
